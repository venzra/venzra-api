-----

## What is Venzra

Venzra is a commerce platform for businesses. Our focus is on getting you and your business online in the simplest possible way. Our customers are able to manage their online business quickly and with minimal technical knowledge.

To get an account online and able to take orders can be achieved in around 5 minutes, although some steps may require gathering information if it is not to hand.

## 1. Creating a new account

To get your store running you can follow these steps;

1. Visit our website and [create an account](https://www.venzra.com/register).
2. Open your email client an look for an email from Venzra (this can take a few minutes depending on your email provider).
3. Follow the link in your email to verify and activate your account, to do this you will need to enter a password to use, [learn more about best practices for passwords](https://www.cyberaware.gov.uk/passwords).

## 2. Create and organise products

1. Log in on the [administration console](https://admin.venzra.com).
2. Create products by providing images, descriptions and pricing information.
3. If you want you can create categories and add products to these categories to help visitors to the store find your products.

## 3. Design your site branding

1. Give your store a name and setup the domain.
2. Upload a logo for your business, if you need a logo we recommend [99 designs](https://www.99designs.co.uk) to help you get started.
3. Choose a colour that best defines your brand.

## 4. Attach a payment gateway

1. In order to get paid you need to connect with a payment gateway, we have partnered with [Stripe](https://www.stripe.com) to process payments with low transaction rates and global coverage.
2. Stripe may require additional proof of your company or legal entity whom will be receiving money for fraud purposes. Don't worry they will get in contact with you if this is the case and typically is for companies who are managing very large volumes of orders.
3. Take a look at the documentation on Stripe and have a look at the dashboard, while most features on Stripe will be available via Venzra there are some process flows you may consider performing via Stripe.

## 5. Wait for your first order and market your online store

1. Contact existing customers and let them know they can now place orders online.
2. Update business cards and stationary with a link to your online store.
3. Give new customers a card when they make purchases so they know they can make repeat orders online with ease.
